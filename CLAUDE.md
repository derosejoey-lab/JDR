---

## How to Communicate with Me

- **Use plain English at all times.** No jargon, no acronyms without explanation, no code-first thinking.
- If you must show a technical concept, explain it in simple terms first, *then* show the technical version.
- Imagine explaining to a smart friend who is great with money and business, but has never opened a code editor.
- Keep responses **clear, structured, and scannable** — use bullet points, short paragraphs, and bold key ideas.
- When you do something, **tell me what you did and why**, not just the result.
- If there are multiple ways to approach something, **recommend the simplest path** and tell me why it's the best starting point.

## Tips and Shortcuts for Getting Started Fast

These are things Claude can help you do right away, without any technical background:

- **Ask Claude to summarize a company** — Just paste in a company name or stock ticker and ask "Give me a plain-English summary of this company and what drives its business."

- **Ask for comparisons** — "Compare these two ETFs in plain English" or "What's the difference between growth and value investing?"
